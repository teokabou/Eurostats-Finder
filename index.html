Frequently Asked Questions

What is Eurostat's Finder?

Eurostat's Finder is a powerful application designed to extract valuable knowledge, including catalogs, datasets, and metadata, directly from Eurostat APIs. It simplifies accessing complex data.

How does it search for information?

The application intelligently queries Eurostat APIs based on your specific input, such as topics or time ranges, to pinpoint the exact information you need.

What results can I expect?

You'll initially see relevant catalogs and datasets matching your search criteria. The application then allows you to retrieve this information along with its metadata.

Can data be in RDF format?

Absolutely! The retrieved data can be transformed into a semantic RDF format, leveraging the available schema and metadata for advanced data representation.

Who can benefit from this tool?

Researchers, analysts, and data scientists at organizations like the European Central Bank or national statistical offices can greatly benefit from streamlined data access.
